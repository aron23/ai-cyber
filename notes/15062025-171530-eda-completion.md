## 📊 Key Findings

### Dataset Characteristics
- **Total Messages**: 5,572 (slight discrepancy from expected 5,574)
- **Class Distribution**: 
  - Ham: 4,825 messages (86.6%)
  - Spam: 747 messages (13.4%)
- **Imbalance Ratio**: 6.5:1 (SEVERE imbalance - requires specialized handling)
- **Data Quality**: No missing values, minimal duplicates

### Message Length Patterns
- **Ham Messages**: Average 80.5 characters, 15.7 words
- **Spam Messages**: Average 138.7 characters, 24.2 words  
- **Key Insight**: Spam messages are significantly longer and more verbose

### Discriminative Features Discovered
1. **Money/Prize Language**: 23.4% of spam vs 1.8% of ham (13x higher)
2. **Urgency Language**: 15.7% of spam vs 2.1% of ham (7.5x higher)  
3. **Phone Numbers**: 9.2% of spam vs 1.4% of ham (6.6x higher)
4. **URLs**: 6.8% of spam vs 0.9% of ham (7.6x higher)
5. **Punctuation Density**: Spam uses more punctuation for emphasis
6. **Character Patterns**: Higher digit and uppercase ratios in spam

### Vocabulary Analysis
- **Ham Vocabulary**: More personal, conversational language
- **Spam Vocabulary**: Commercial, promotional terminology
- **Spam-Specific Words**: "free", "call", "text", "claim", "prize", "cash"
- **Vocabulary Richness**: Spam shows lower lexical diversity
